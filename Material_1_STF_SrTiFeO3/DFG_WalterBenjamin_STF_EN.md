2 Programme fit and strategy

The Walter Benjamin Programme is the DFG-native alternative to the Lynen fellowship: the identical 24-month work programme is funded as a fellowship abroad, and the subsequent return funding bridges directly into the Emmy Noether application – reviewers then see the entire path (pilot → return → group) within one funding organisation, with consistent terminology and the COMET-Ox proposal as the declared perspective.

3 Project core (summary)

Covalency as an experimental descriptor of intrinsic memristive switching in SrTi1-xFexO3-δ (STF) – the only perovskite series in which intrinsic Pt-interface switching, a graphene-sealed spectroscopy platform and a closed-form defect chemistry (Eg(x), ΔHred(x); Rothschild 2006) already coexist. The project grows the Fe composition series, measures the XAS covalency scale at SSRL and correlates it with the switching statistics of the same cells; stoichiometry-controlled switching at a Pt interface is already demonstrated for the Sr/Ti cation axis (Mikheev 2015) and serves as the methodological blueprint.

Materials selection follows the portfolio’s three-criterion evidence logic (covalency vs. stoichiometry by XAS; EV vs. covalency; demonstrated switching at a noble-metal interface); all references of the underlying documents are individually verified against publisher records (see Literaturpruefung_2026-08-09 and Materialscreening_2026-08-09).
